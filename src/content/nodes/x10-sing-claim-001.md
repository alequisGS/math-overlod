## Statement

For a quasismooth degree-ten hypersurface
$X_{10} \subset \mathbf P(1,2,3,5)$, the theorem identifies a unique singular
point $p$ of type $\frac13(1,1)$.

## Scope

This claim records the memberwise singularity assertion in the theorem. The record `X10-SING-001` describes the local type itself; this claim asserts that $X_{10}$ has exactly one point of that type. It does not assert that every degree-ten hypersurface is quasismooth or has this singularity.

## About

This claim is about the surface record `X10-001` and the singularity-type record `X10-SING-001`. Its documentary fragment and reviewed attestation are listed below the statement.
