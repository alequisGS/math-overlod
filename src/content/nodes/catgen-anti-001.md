## Definition

Given a bilinear Euler pairing, use the convention

$$\chi^-(v,w)=\chi(v,w)-\chi(w,v).$$

This is an alternating form on the underlying abelian group. Dividing by two is a different convention and is not used here.

## Why it matters

Separating the skew part makes the proposed genus direction a concrete question about numerical categorical data.
