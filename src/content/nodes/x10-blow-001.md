## Description

Let $\mathbb F_3=\mathbf P_{\mathbf P^1}(\mathcal O\oplus\mathcal O(3))$. The notation $\operatorname{Bl}_8(\mathbb F_3)$ means the blow-up at eight specified points. Their locations and incidence conditions matter.

This entry records the model named in the research outline. A precise identification with $\widetilde X_{10}$, including the point configuration and morphism, is left to the source rather than asserted for arbitrary eight points.

## Why it matters

An explicit birational model can turn abstract categorical existence questions into geometric constructions.
