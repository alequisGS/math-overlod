## Statement

For the surface $X_{10}$ and its canonical stack $\mathcal X_{10}$ in the cited work, the category $D^b(\operatorname{coh}\mathcal X_{10})$ admits a full exceptional collection.

This is the headline result supplied for the atlas. The precise hypotheses, construction and proof belong to the cited preprint. This page is a stable entry for the result, not a replacement for its proof.

## Why it matters

The result makes the category accessible through finitely many exceptional generators and opens a route to its Euler form.
