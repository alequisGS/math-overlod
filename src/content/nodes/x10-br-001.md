## Description

For a scheme or stack $Y$, the cohomological Brauer group is commonly written

$$\operatorname{Br}^{\prime}(Y)=H^2_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(Y,\mathbf G_m)_{\mathrm{tors}}.$$

Its comparison with the Azumaya Brauer group requires hypotheses. The beta records the Brauer direction in the supplied research; it does not infer a value for every surface or stack from the notation alone.

## Why it matters

Brauer classes can detect twisting that would be invisible in an unqualified list of exceptional objects.
