## Description

For a chosen ordered exceptional collection $(E_i)$, its Euler matrix has entries

$$G_{ij}=\chi(E_i,E_j)=\sum_m(-1)^m\dim\operatorname{Ext}^m(E_i,E_j).$$

The source reports an Euler-form calculation. No numerical matrix or reproducible calculation is included here; order and sign conventions must be checked against the paper.

## Why it matters

The pairing provides the conceptual passage from the concrete surface to the categorical genus program.
