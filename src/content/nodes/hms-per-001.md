## Description

A typical period has the form

$$\Pi(t)=\int_{\gamma_t}\omega_t,$$

where the form and cycle belong to an appropriate varying geometric family. Choices of family, cycles, branches and normalization are essential. No specific differential equation is attached to X₁₀ here.

## Why it matters

Periods offer a tractable numerical and analytic view of geometry, with links to monodromy and arithmetic.
