## Description

Denote the minimal resolution by $\pi:\widetilde X_{10}\to X_{10}$. This is a proper birational morphism from a smooth surface, an isomorphism over the smooth locus, with no exceptional $(-1)$-curve that can be contracted while remaining a resolution.

## Why it matters

A smooth resolution gives access to geometric constructions of exceptional collections before comparison with the canonical stack.
