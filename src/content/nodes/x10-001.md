## Description

The research object is a suitable quasismooth log del Pezzo hypersurface

$$X_{10} \subset \mathbf P(1,2,3,5), \qquad \deg X_{10}=10.$$

The weights specify the ambient space, not a unique equation. The beta does not choose coefficients or claim that every degree-ten equation has the required properties.

## Why it matters

The surface is the concrete test case connecting resolution geometry, exceptional collections, Euler pairings and a prospective mirror direction.
