## Description

A quotient singularity is locally modeled by a quotient of a smooth space by a finite group. The action, dimension and base-field characteristic are part of the data. The associated stack retains stabilizers that are not retained as objects of the coarse space.

## Why it matters

These models connect the local geometry of X₁₀ to broader categorical examples.
