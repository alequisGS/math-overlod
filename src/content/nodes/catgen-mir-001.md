## Description

After fixing a definition of categorical genus, seek a mirror interpretation compatible with the relevant categorical and period data. This is a question about a future invariant, not an established correspondence or a specific conjectural equality.

## Why it matters

A mirror interpretation could explain why a categorical invariant should reflect geometric structure.
