## Statement

The coarse surface $X_{10}$ admits the semiorthogonal decomposition

$$
D^b(\operatorname{coh}X_{10})=
\left\langle D^b(K(3,1)\text{-mod}),F_1,\dots,F_{10}\right\rangle,
$$

where
$K(3,1)\cong\mathbb C[z_1,z_2]/(z_1,z_2)^2$ and $F_1,\dots,F_{10}$ are exceptional objects made explicit in Theorem `thm:X10-kks-sod`.

## Scope

This claim concerns the specific coarse surface $X_{10}$ and the displayed decomposition in item (ii) of Theorem 1.3. It does not identify the result with the general KKS mechanism recorded by `X10-KKS-001`, and it does not make a claim about the canonical stack category.

## About

This claim is about the surface record `X10-001`, the KKS construction record `X10-KKS-001`, and the semiorthogonal-decomposition record `X10-SOD-001`. Its documentary fragment and reviewed attestation are listed below the statement.
