## Description

Homological mirror symmetry seeks equivalences between suitable algebraic and symplectic categories attached to mirror partners. The categories and hypotheses depend on the setting. The beta records this research program without asserting a homological mirror equivalence for $X_{10}$.

## Why it matters

The program provides a context in which categorical structure and period calculations may inform one another.
