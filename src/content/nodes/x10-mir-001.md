## Description

A prospective Laurent-polynomial mirror has the form $W\in\mathbf C[x^{\pm1},y^{\pm1}]$. This node asks which candidate and comparison conventions are appropriate for $X_{10}$. No specific polynomial, period equality or homological equivalence is claimed.

## Why it matters

A mirror candidate would connect explicit period calculations to categorical questions, while remaining distinct from a proved equivalence.
