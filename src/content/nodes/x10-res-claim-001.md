## Statement

For a quasismooth degree-ten hypersurface
$X_{10} \subset \mathbf P(1,2,3,5)$, its minimal resolution admits a presentation as the blow-up of $\mathbb F_3$ at eight distinct points away from $C_0$.

## Scope

This claim records the memberwise resolution presentation. It does not assert that arbitrary eight points away from $C_0$ produce such a hypersurface, that the presentation is unique, or that the generality comparison has been proved here.
