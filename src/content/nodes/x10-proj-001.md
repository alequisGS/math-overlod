## Description

The central example is a degree-ten hypersurface $X_{10} \subset \mathbf P(1,2,3,5)$. The program follows its geometry through a resolution and a canonical stack, and then studies exceptional collections and numerical invariants.

## Why it matters

One geometric object brings several methods into contact. Its categorical calculations also motivate questions about genus and mirrors.
