## Definition

Over $\mathbf C$, the notation $\frac13(1,1)$ denotes the germ of $\mathbf A^2/\mu_3$ at the image of the origin, where

$$\zeta\cdot(x,y)=(\zeta x,\zeta y), \qquad \zeta^3=1.$$

This is a local analytic model; it does not specify the global surface.

## Why it matters

The local singularity type controls which resolution and stack constructions enter the research program.
