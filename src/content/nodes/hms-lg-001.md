## Description

A Landau–Ginzburg model consists of a space $Y$ and a function $W:Y\to\mathbf A^1$, together with any additional structure required by the chosen theory. A Laurent polynomial on an algebraic torus is one class of examples.

## Why it matters

The potential gives an explicit object on which period and categorical questions can be asked.
