## Description

The proposed program considers smooth proper Deligne–Mumford stacks with an appropriate positivity condition on the anticanonical bundle. Precise conventions for ampleness on stacks and the class of examples are still to be specified. No general exceptional-collection theorem is asserted.

## Why it matters

Moving from one surface to a class of stacks tests which categorical phenomena are structural.
