## Definition

A semiorthogonal decomposition $\mathcal D=\langle\mathcal A_1,\ldots,\mathcal A_r\rangle$ consists of full triangulated subcategories generating $\mathcal D$ with $\operatorname{Hom}(A_j,A_i)=0$ for $j>i$ and all $A_j\in\mathcal A_j$, $A_i\in\mathcal A_i$.

## Why it matters

It records categorical structure beyond a list of objects and makes the direction of orthogonality explicit.
