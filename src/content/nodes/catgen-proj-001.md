## Description

The program has three mathematical tasks: specify the numerical data and conventions; propose a precise invariant; and test its behavior on families such as $\mathbf P(1,1,n)$ and suitable Fano stacks. None of these steps is marked complete merely by inclusion in the graph.

## Why it matters

The project keeps prospective generalizations separate from the proved X₁₀ result that motivates them.
