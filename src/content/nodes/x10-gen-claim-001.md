## Statement

A general quasismooth degree-ten hypersurface $X_{10}$ is the anticanonical model of the blow-up of $\mathbb P(1,1,3)$ at eight general smooth points, and conversely general centers give a general hypersurface.

## Scope

This claim is the two-way generality comparison in item (iii) of Theorem 1.3. Both directions are restricted to general members and general smooth centers. It does not assert uniqueness of the centers, an isomorphism of parameter spaces, that arbitrary centers produce arbitrary hypersurfaces, or that every member has this generality property.

## About

This claim is about the surface record `X10-001` and the blow-up-model record `X10-BLOW-001`. It is a generality statement and remains distinct from the memberwise eight-point resolution claim. Its documentary fragment and reviewed attestation are listed below the statement.
